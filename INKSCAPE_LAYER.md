# Inkscape control layer

Layer 3 is dedicated to Inkscape.

| Row | Key 1 | Key 2 | Key 3 | Key 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Select (`S`) | Node (`N`) | Settings (hold) | Numpad |
| 2 | Duplicate | Group | Ungroup | Object to Path |
| 3 | Union | Difference | Intersection | Break Apart |
| 4 | Combine | Break Apart | Raise | Lower |
| 5 | Fill & Stroke | Align & Distribute | Layers & Objects | Export |
| 6 | Zoom Selection | Zoom Drawing | Save | Undo |

## Encoder

On the Inkscape layer:

- clockwise: zoom in (`+`)
- counter-clockwise: zoom out (`-`)
- press: zoom to selection (`3`)

On all other layers, the encoder keeps its previous mouse-wheel behavior and pressing it toggles RGB.

## Layer access

Open Settings and use the existing `TO(3)` key to enter the Inkscape layer. The Inkscape layer has its own blue RGB hue.
